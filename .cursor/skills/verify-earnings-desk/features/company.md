# Inspect a company

## Sub-features

Select a reporter; read its growth, reaction, quoted headline and source; understand revised comparisons.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the dashboard and click Honeywell in Reported.

## Driving it with IAB

Click `#rep-table tbody tr[data-key="HON"]`. Verify `#d-title` names Honeywell and `#detail` says the year-ago comparison changed. Verify the previous-quarter growth reads `last quarter —`, the table's acceleration is `—`, and the source link points to SEC. Check the headline, outlook and reaction against the saved row; a reaction remains `too early` until both sessions have closed. Save a screenshot of this result.

## Gotchas

The October 22 snapshot withheld Honeywell. This journey requires a build with the October 22 10-Q comparison available; report a missing precondition rather than substituting fabricated data.
